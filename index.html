About the Doctor

Experienced, Internationally Trained Urologist

Prof. Dr. Muhammad Haroon Ghous is a urologist practicing in Lahore, Pakistan, with training from the Royal Colleges of Surgeons of Edinburgh and Ireland and advanced minimally invasive surgical training at the University of Strasbourg, France.

He treats a wide range of urological conditions with a focus on modern endoscopic techniques, cancer care, and men's sexual health, in a respectful and confidential setting.
