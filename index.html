About the Doctor

Over 25 Years of Urology Experience

Prof. Dr. Muhammad Haroon Ghous is a urologist, andrologist and uro-oncologist practicing in Lahore, Pakistan. He graduated MBBS from the University of the Punjab in 1994 and trained with the Royal Colleges of Surgeons of Edinburgh and Ireland, with advanced minimally invasive surgical training at the University of Strasbourg, France.

He treats a wide range of urological conditions with a focus on modern endoscopic techniques, cancer care, and men's sexual health, in a respectful and confidential setting.

25+years of hands-on experience in urology, treating thousands of patients across Lahore.
